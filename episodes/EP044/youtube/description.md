Every Type of Investor Explained (and Who Wins)

Everyone calls themselves 'an investor' — but there are a dozen wildly different species, from the patient value investor to the frantic day trader. This is every type of investor explained, by the numbers: value (Graham, Buffett), growth, index (Bogle), income/dividends, momentum, day trading, macro (Soros), activism (Icahn), and pure speculation (Livermore) — all on one spectrum from investing to gambling. Then the brutal data on who actually wins: the patient, low-cost, long-term crowd. Fully cited.
