Why Japan Is Stuck (and Still Rich Anyway)

One of the richest, safest countries on Earth has barely grown in thirty years. This is Japan's paradox, by the numbers: the biggest asset bubble in history, the 1989 Nikkei peak that took 34 years to reclaim, the Lost Decades of deflation and zero interest rates, zombie firms, an aging and shrinking population, and a government debt near 250% of GDP — and yet a society that stayed wealthy, healthy, and remarkably stable. Why it's stuck, why it's still rich, and why it may be a preview of the whole aging rich world. Fully cited.
