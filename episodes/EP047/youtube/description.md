What Happens If the Dollar Loses Reserve Status

Every day, the US does something no other country can: spend more than it earns, borrow trillions cheaply, and print the money the whole world needs — all because the dollar is the global reserve currency. This is what happens if it loses that status, by the numbers: the four perks of the 'exorbitant privilege,' why it's eroding, the scenario if it slips (spiking rates, inflation, lost sanctions power), the Triffin dilemma, the British pound precedent, and why — with no replacement ready — it would be a slow fade, not a crash.
