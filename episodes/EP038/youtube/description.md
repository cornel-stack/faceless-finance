The Petrodollar: America's Most Powerful Weapon

America's most powerful weapon isn't its military — it's a 1970s deal about how the world buys oil. This is the petrodollar, by the numbers: how Nixon cut the dollar from gold in 1971, how a US–Saudi bargain backed it with oil instead, how that made the dollar the world's reserve currency, and how it lets America borrow trillions and switch off an enemy's economy with sanctions. Plus the de-dollarization backlash — and whether the whole system is quietly cracking. Fully cited.
