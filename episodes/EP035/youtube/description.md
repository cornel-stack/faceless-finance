Why Markets Panic When Central Banks Speak

A quiet official reads a few careful sentences — and trillions of dollars move within seconds. No new law, no product, just words. This is why markets panic when central banks speak, by the numbers: how the Fed sets the price of money, why markets trade the future instead of today, how 'forward guidance' and 'Fed speak' turn talk into a weapon, and the famous lines that made history — Volcker's resolve, Greenspan's 'irrational exuberance,' Bernanke's taper tantrum, and Draghi's 'whatever it takes.' Fully cited.
