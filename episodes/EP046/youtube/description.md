Why the Era of US Dominance Is (Mathematically) Ending

The United States has about 4% of the world's people — but a quarter of its entire economy. For 80 years that's felt like a law of nature. It isn't. This is why the era of US dominance is (mathematically) ending, by the numbers: the post-WW2 peak at ~40% of world GDP, the force of 'convergence' (poorer economies grow faster), China's purchasing-power crossing, the shift to a multipolar world — and the honest caveats about where America still rules. Not collapse. Catch-up. Fully cited.
