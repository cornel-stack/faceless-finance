Theranos: The $700M That Vanished

Elizabeth Holmes raised more than $700 million and hit a $9 billion valuation on one promise: hundreds of medical tests from a single drop of blood. The problem? The machine didn't work — and the people running Theranos knew. This is the anatomy of Silicon Valley's most famous fraud, by the numbers: the staged demos, the commercial machines it secretly used, the real patients who got voided results, the whistleblowers and the reporter who broke it, and the collapse from $9 billion to $0. Fully cited.
