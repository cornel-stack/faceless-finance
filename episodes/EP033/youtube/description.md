Why the Rich Pretend to Be Poor

Some of the richest people alive drive old cars, wear the same T-shirt, and report less taxable income than a schoolteacher — and it's not an accident. This is why the rich pretend to be poor, by the numbers: the difference between income and wealth, the $1 salaries, untaxed unrealized gains, the 'buy, borrow, die' strategy, and the frugal-billionaire flex from Buffett to Sam Walton. One study found the 25 richest paid a 'true tax rate' near 3%. Here's exactly how — all legal, all cited.
