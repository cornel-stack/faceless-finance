Why Gold Is Doing Something It Has Never Done

Gold just hit the highest price in human history — and it did it by breaking a rule it had followed for 50 years. Normally, when real interest rates rise, gold falls, because gold pays no yield. Lately rates spiked, the dollar was strong, and gold should have been crushed. Instead it soared to records. This is why, by the numbers: central banks (after $300B of Russia's reserves were frozen in 2022) are buying gold at the fastest pace in history as an un-freezable, anti-dollar hedge — even as Western investors sold.
