Wirecard: The $2 Billion That Never Existed

Wirecard was Germany's proudest tech company — a €24 billion fintech star in the DAX index, right next to Siemens and BMW. Then it admitted that €1.9 billion of its cash, a quarter of its balance sheet, probably never existed. This is the Wirecard scandal, by the numbers: the fake Asian profits, the empty escrow accounts, the short sellers and Financial Times journalists who were right for years, the regulator that attacked them instead, the collapse of the first-ever DAX member, and a fugitive COO who vanished and is now a suspected spy.
