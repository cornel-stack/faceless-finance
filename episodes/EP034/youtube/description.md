The Fastest Way to Get Poor (the Data)

Getting rich is slow. Getting poor can take an afternoon. This is the data on the fastest ways to destroy wealth — and the hidden math they all share. Gambler's ruin makes casino losses a mathematical certainty. The lottery has worse odds than being struck by lightning twice. Payday loans hit 400% APR. And leverage can turn a 10% dip into a 100% wipeout. We break down each one, then the boring playbook that makes getting poor fast surprisingly hard to do. Fully cited.
