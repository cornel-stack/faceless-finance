Lehman Brothers: The 72 Hours That Broke the World

Friday, September 12, 2008: a 158-year-old Wall Street titan is standing. By Monday it's gone — the biggest bankruptcy in US history, and the global financial system is in cardiac arrest. This is the Lehman Brothers collapse, by the numbers: the subprime machine, mortgage-backed securities and CDOs stamped AAA, 30-to-1 leverage, the failed rescue weekend, the $600 billion bankruptcy, the credit freeze, the $700 billion bailout, and the 'too big to fail' dilemma we never really solved.
