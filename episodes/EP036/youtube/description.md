How Scammers Rig the Stock Market

The stock market looks like a fair auction — but underneath runs a whole playbook of tricks to tilt the game before you place a bet. This is how scammers rig the market, by the numbers: pump-and-dumps and boiler rooms, high-speed spoofing and wash trading, short-and-distort, insider trading, front-running, and the 2010 flash crash that wiped out a trillion dollars in 36 minutes. Then the simple rules that keep you from being the exit liquidity. Fully cited.

• Front-running and the 2010 flash crash
• Four rules to avoid being the one holding the bag
