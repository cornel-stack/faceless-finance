How Debt Became the Most Profitable Product in History

The most profitable product ever invented isn't oil, the iPhone, or AI. It's debt. This is how debt became the ultimate product, by the numbers: you sell money and get more back (interest), the raw material is created from almost nothing (fractional-reserve banking), you pocket 'the spread' between what you pay savers and charge borrowers, you securitize the loans to lend the same dollar again and again, it compounds forever, and it's now wrapped around everything — homes, cars, cards, degrees, and governments. The genius, the dark side, and which side of the debt you want to be on. Fully cited.

IN THIS VIDEO
• Why selling money beats selling any physical product
• Fractional-reserve banking: raw material created from nothing
• 'The spread' (net interest margin) — the core of bank profit
• Securitization: how banks lend the same dollar again and again
• Compounding — a money machine for lenders, a trap for borrowers
• The lesson: be the one collecting interest, not paying it
