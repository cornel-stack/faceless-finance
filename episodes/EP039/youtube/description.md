Why the Dumbest Investors Win

A gas-station attendant left $8 million. Meanwhile, armies of brilliant Wall Street pros lose to a strategy so simple it feels insulting: buy a cheap index fund and never touch it. This is why the dumbest investors win, by the numbers — why ~90% of active funds underperform a basic index over 15 years, how fees, the efficient market, and our own panic sabotage the 'smart money,' Warren Buffett's million-dollar bet that proved it, and the boring playbook anyone can follow. Fully cited.
